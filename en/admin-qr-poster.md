# QR Poster

Create a **QR poster** for on-site display (photo report submission QR).

## How to use it

1. Generate the poster in the admin screen.
2. Print it and post it at the site.
3. Craftsmen scan the QR code with their smartphone and can start reporting from LINE.

Handy for introducing the service at new sites or to subcontractors' craftsmen.
