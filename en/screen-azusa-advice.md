# Azusa Advice

Azusa, the AI assistant, replies with **advice for working safely** (risk-prediction advice) based on your project information.

## Getting advice

Two ways:

1. **Send project information** — via a free-form message or the project info form; Azusa replies with advice, and you can continue straight into creating a report (see [Photo Reports](screen-photo-report.html)).
2. **From the rich menu** — tap "Azusa Advice" to open the Report List, then tap "Select" on the report you want advice about.
   - Reports without a project name can't receive advice (their "Select" button is disabled).

The advice is a short message with concrete safety measures, always ending with a "safety first" sign-off.

## Consultation mode

If you send a question or concern rather than project information, Azusa switches to **consultation mode** and answers construction-related questions with AI. Answers may include references to relevant regulations and safety standards.

- No report is created in consultation mode.
- While the AI is thinking, a loading animation is shown in the chat.
