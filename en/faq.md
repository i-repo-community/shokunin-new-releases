# FAQ & Troubleshooting

## Registration & getting started

**Q. I registered but can't use the features.**
A. Right after registering, your account is "pending approval". Once an administrator approves you, everything unlocks — Azusa will notify you when that happens.

**Q. Which languages are supported?**
A. Japanese and English. The rich menu is displayed in Japanese only, but your messages are recognized in either language.

**Q. Do I need to install an app?**
A. No. Craftsmen only need the LINE app; supervisors use the admin screen in a browser.

## Reports

**Q. I want to replace or delete a photo in a report.**
A. Replacing or deleting existing photos isn't supported. Use "Rebuild" to create a new report that carries over the basic information, then send the photos again.

**Q. What happens when I delete a report?**
A. All photos attached to the report are deleted too. This cannot be undone, so read the confirmation message carefully.

**Q. My download link no longer opens.**
A. Downloads have an expiry. Open the download screen again via "Download" in the Report List.

**Q. Can I share a report with someone who doesn't use LINE?**
A. Yes — share the download screen's URL by email or any other way, and they can download the PDF in their browser.

## Admin screen

**Q. I can't log in to the admin screen.**
A. The admin screen uses LINE Login. On a PC, scan the QR code shown with the LINE app on your smartphone. If you still can't get in, contact your tenant's administrator.
