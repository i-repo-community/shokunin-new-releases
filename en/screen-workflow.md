# Workflows

Workflows let you **report along a defined procedure**. You fill in the steps your supervisor defined as a form, so nothing gets missed.

## How it works

1. Start the workflow from the "work start" card that arrives in the chat.
2. Follow the on-screen steps, adding photos and information at each one.
3. Finish all steps and submit — a report is created.

## Good to know

- The steps and fields are defined by your supervisor in the admin screen (Workflow Definitions), so they differ per site and per job.
- The resulting report appears in the [Report List](screen-report-list.html) like any other photo report — you can edit and download it the same way.
