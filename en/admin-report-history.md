# Report History

Review the reports craftsmen have submitted.

## What you can do

- Browse all submitted reports in a list.
- Switch between each report's **details** and its **PDF** with tabs; the PDF can also open in a new tab.
- Workflow reports are listed with their form name, revision, and submitter.

Check report contents from the office in real time — no site visit needed.
