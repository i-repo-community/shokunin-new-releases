# Download

Finished reports can be downloaded as PDFs. Choose "Download" on a report in the Report List to open the download screen.

## What the download screen offers

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| Thumbnail & info | Review the report's contents |
| Download | Download the report PDF (file name is "project name + report date") |
| QR code | The screen's URL as a QR code — tap to enlarge or save |
| Open in browser | Open this screen in the device's browser |
| Email | Opens your mail app with the download URL pasted in |
| Share | Opens LINE's forward menu |

## Signing

You can sign the report on the download screen (the screen title is "Sign Report").

1. Draw your signature in the box with your finger. The "Sign & complete" button stays disabled until you've signed.
2. Tap "Sign & complete" to save the signature and complete the report. To redo it before tapping, tap "Clear".
3. Once signed, the screen shows "This report has been signed" and your signature. To sign again, tap "Re-sign".

- If you tap "Download PDF", email or share while your signature is drawn but not yet saved, it is saved automatically.
- Your location is recorded with the signature. If you're asked for location access the first time you sign, please allow it.
- Depending on your company's settings, you may see "Signature pending admin approval" after signing; the report is completed once an admin approves it.
- While your signature is being saved, "Download PDF", email and share can't be tapped until saving finishes (on photo reports the button shows "Updating PDF…"). After that, the latest PDF with your signature opens.
- If you see "Your signature is saved. The PDF is still being updated", your signature is saved but isn't on the PDF yet. Please try again in a moment.

### Your signature on the PDF

Your signature is printed on the photo report PDF, in the **"作業コメント" (work comment) box on page 2**.

- It is printed while the signature is pending admin approval, too.
- If you tap "Re-sign", the PDF shows the new signature.
- If an admin rejects or deletes the signature, it is removed from the PDF.
- Page 2's work comment box holds the signature; the work comment itself still appears on page 1 and the other pages.
- For reports signed before this feature was available, the signature is printed the next time the report is updated.
- It is not printed on workflow report PDFs.

## Good to know

- Share the download URL to hand the report to people who don't use LINE (the office, the ordering party, and so on).
- The URL contains an encrypted ID, so it can't be guessed by third parties.
- Downloads have an expiry and a usage limit; if a link expires, open the download screen again from the Report List.
