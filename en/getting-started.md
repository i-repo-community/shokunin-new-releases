# Getting Started

Shokunin.new is an assistant service for craftsmen: send photos over LINE and get a finished construction photo report. Azusa, the AI assistant, supports you along the way.

## For craftsmen (LINE)

1. **Add the LINE official account**
   Add the Shokunin.new LINE official account as a friend. (QR code: coming soon)
2. **Register**
   Follow the guidance in the chat to register your name and profile.
3. **Send photos to create a report**
   Send photos taken on-site into the chat and a report is created. Check, edit, and download the PDF from the "Report List" in the menu.

## For supervisors (admin screen)

- Log in to the admin screen from your browser. (The URL is provided with your contract.)
- The dashboard shows reporting status, and you can manage dispatch and define workflows.
